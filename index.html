Postdoodle Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 September 2026. Postdoodle is published by AppLab Studio ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the Postdoodle Android app collects, why, and how you can delete it.

What Postdoodle is

Postdoodle is a private room for two people. You draw doodles or answer questions, your partner guesses when they next open the app, and the winner banks a small wish in a shared list. There are no ads and no public feed. Everything you send is visible only to the one person in your room.

Data we collect

  • Anonymous account ID. On first launch the app creates a random account ID. No email, phone number or password is required. We do not know who you are.
  • Display name and language. The name you type (up to 24 characters) is shown to your partner. Your language choice (English or Hinglish) is stored with it.
  • Room and rounds. Your room code, the doodles you draw (stroke data), the questions you answer, guesses, and the wishes you write. These are shared with your partner in the room and with nobody else.
  • Usage events. A small set of product events (for example "round sent", "wish banked", "account deleted") with timestamps, tied to your anonymous account ID. They contain no message content and no personal details. We use them to see whether the app works and which parts people use.
  • Crash reports. If we add crash reporting, it will collect device model, OS version and the technical error, never message content. This policy will be updated before that happens.

We do not collect your contacts, location, photos, files, advertising ID or device identifiers. The app requests no Android permissions.

Where data is stored

Data is stored on Supabase (supabase.com) servers in the Mumbai, India region, protected by row-level security so that only the two members of a room can read that room. Data travels over HTTPS only. Your session token is kept in your phone's encrypted storage.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your data with advertisers or data brokers. Supabase processes it as our hosting provider. If you use the "Share invite" button, your phone's share sheet sends the invite text (your name and the room code) to whichever app you choose; that app's privacy policy applies to it.

Deleting your data

  • Leave room (Settings) deletes the room, every round and every wish for both partners immediately.
  • Delete account (Settings) deletes your account, your profile, everything you sent and your usage events immediately and permanently. Your partner's own sent rounds in a room you owned are deleted with the room.

Because accounts are anonymous, we cannot restore a deleted account. If you cannot open the app, email us at the address below with your room code and we will delete the room and its data.

Children

Postdoodle is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.
